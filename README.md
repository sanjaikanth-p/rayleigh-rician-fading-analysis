# Performance Analysis of Wireless Communication Systems over Rayleigh and Rician Fading Channels Using MATLAB

### Simulation and Analysis of Wireless Channel Fading Models

A MATLAB-based academic project exploring the performance characteristics of wireless communication systems under Rayleigh and Rician fading conditions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Wireless communication signals experience variations in amplitude and phase due to multipath propagation and environmental conditions.

This project explores the performance of wireless communication systems under two commonly studied fading models:

- Rayleigh Fading
- Rician Fading

MATLAB is used as the simulation environment to study the characteristics of these channels and understand their effects on wireless signal transmission.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Study Rayleigh and Rician fading models.
- Understand the effects of multipath propagation.
- Explore wireless channel behaviour through MATLAB simulations.
- Analyse the characteristics of different fading environments.
- Develop a foundational understanding of wireless communication performance.

## 📡 Concepts Explored

### 1. Rayleigh Fading

Rayleigh fading is a statistical model commonly used to represent wireless communication environments where multiple reflected signal components contribute to the received signal without a dominant line-of-sight component.

### 2. Rician Fading

Rician fading represents environments where a dominant signal component, such as a line-of-sight path, exists alongside multiple reflected components.

### 3. Wireless Channel Analysis

The project explores how different propagation environments influence received wireless signals.
